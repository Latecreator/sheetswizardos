Budgeting & Break-Even

The Reality Check:
How Many Sales Until You Actually Make Money

=A2/(B2-C2)

Your fixed costs, divided by your profit per sale. One number that tells you exactly when you stop losing money.

For Solopreneurs & Creators

You're about to launch a £19 Excel template. You've spent £85 on design tools, hosting, and a Stan Store subscription. Payment processing takes £1.20 per sale.

Fixed Costs (A2) £85
Price Per Unit (B2) £19.00
Variable Cost (C2) £1.20
Break-Even Units 5 sales

Five sales. That's it. Suddenly launching that template doesn't feel like a gamble — it feels like a plan.

For Office & Small Business

Your team wants to run a training workshop. The room hire, materials, and catering cost £2,400. You're charging £120 per head, with £15 in per-person costs.

Fixed Costs (A2) £2,400
Price Per Unit (B2) £120.00
Variable Cost (C2) £15.00
Break-Even Units 23 attendees

Now you know: fewer than 23 sign-ups and you're subsidising the event. More than 23 and every extra ticket is pure profit.
