Find Your Top Performer Instantly

=INDEX(A2:A10, MATCH(MAX(B2:B10), B2:B10, 0))

Stop scrolling through rows to find your top performer. This formula instantly returns the name of whoever — or whatever — has the highest number in your data.

🚀

For Solopreneurs & Creators

You've got a spreadsheet tracking your digital products — templates, ebooks, courses. Each one has a sales column. Instead of scanning every row to see which product is carrying your business, this formula pulls the top seller's name instantly. Use it in your monthly review dashboard to spot your winner without thinking.

Example: =INDEX(Products, MATCH(MAX(Sales), Sales, 0)) → "Budget Planner Template"
🏢

For Office & Small Business

You're running a sales team or managing regional performance. You've got names in one column and figures in another. Instead of sorting and re-sorting every time someone asks "who's top this month?", this formula gives you the answer live. Drop it into your team dashboard and it updates as the numbers come in.

Example: =INDEX(Reps, MATCH(MAX(Revenue), Revenue, 0)) → "Sarah Chen"
