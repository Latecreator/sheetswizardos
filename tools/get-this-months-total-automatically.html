Get This Month's Total Automatically

=SUMPRODUCT((MONTH(A2:A100)=MONTH(TODAY()))*(B2:B100))

Stop filtering by date every time you want this month's total. Put your dates in column A, your amounts in column B, and this formula sums only the current month — automatically.

🚀

For Solopreneurs & Creators

You're logging every sale from your Stan Store, Gumroad, or Etsy shop into a spreadsheet. Each row has a date and an amount. Instead of filtering to this month and manually summing, this formula gives you a live running total that resets automatically when the month changes. Drop it at the top of your dashboard and always know where you stand.

Example: 47 sales logged this month → formula returns £1,245.00
🏢

For Office & Small Business

Your team logs invoices, expenses, or orders with a date column. Your manager asks "what's the total this month?" every week. Instead of filtering, sorting, and re-summing each time, this formula sits in a summary cell and always shows the current month's total — no maintenance, no manual updates, no room for error.

Example: 132 invoices this month → formula returns £28,450.00
