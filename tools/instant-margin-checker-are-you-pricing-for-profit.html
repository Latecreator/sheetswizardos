Pricing & Margins

Instant Margin Checker

Stop accepting bad margins without realising it. This formula shows your exact margin percentage — and flags it if you're underpricing.

=IF((B2-A2)/B2 < C2,
  "❌ " & TEXT((B2-A2)/B2, "0.0%") & " — reprice",
  "✅ " & TEXT((B2-A2)/B2, "0.0%") & " margin")

A2 = cost  ·  B2 = selling price  ·  C2 = target margin (e.g. 0.3 for 30%)

Solopreneur

Pricing a Digital Product

You made an Excel template that cost you £10 in tools/time. You're selling it at £27 and you want at least 60% margin. Enter the numbers — the formula tells you instantly if you're hitting your target or leaving money on the table.

=IF((27-10)/27 < 0.6, "❌ 37.0% — reprice", "✅ 37.0% margin")
→ ❌ 37.0% — reprice
Small Business

Checking Product Pricing

You stock a product at £8.50 cost and sell it at £19.99. Your business needs 50% margin to cover overheads. Run it through the formula — are you actually hitting that number, or is the margin tighter than you thought?

=IF((19.99-8.50)/19.99 < 0.5, "❌ ...", "✅ 57.5% margin")
→ ✅ 57.5% margin

How It Works in Excel

1

(B2-A2)/B2 — calculates your gross margin as a decimal. B2 is selling price, A2 is cost.

2

< C2 — checks if that margin falls below your target (stored as a decimal in C2, e.g. 0.3 for 30%).

3

TEXT(..., "0.0%") — formats the decimal as a readable percentage (e.g. 0.575 → 57.5%).

4

The IF combines everything: if margin is too low → show ❌ + percentage + "reprice". If it's fine → show ✅ + percentage + "margin".
