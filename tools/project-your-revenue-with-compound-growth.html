Growth & Forecasting

Project Your Revenue With Compound Growth

=A2*(1+B2/100)^C2

Put your current revenue in A2, your monthly growth rate (%) in B2, and the number of months in C2. The formula compounds your growth and shows where you'll be.

🚀

For Solopreneurs & Creators

You're earning from digital products, courses, or freelance work and growing month-on-month. Instead of guessing when you can quit your day job, plug in your current monthly revenue and growth rate. This formula shows you exactly when you'll hit your income target — so you can plan your exit with confidence, not hope.

Example: Starting at £500/month with 15% growth → £2,028/month after 10 months
🏢

For Office & Small Business

Your manager wants a revenue forecast for the next 12 months. Instead of building a complex model, use this formula with last quarter's average monthly revenue and your historical growth rate. It gives you a defensible projection in seconds — perfect for board decks, budgets, and capacity planning.

Example: Starting at £10,000/month with 5% growth → £17,959/month after 12 months
